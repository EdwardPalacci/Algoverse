Figure 7. Prompt intervention metrics comparing autoregressive language models (AR) and diffusion language models (DLMs), pooled over each family's generations. ECE is expected calibration error using 10 equal-width confidence bins. AURC is area under the risk-coverage curve, computed as the expected value over orderings of tied confidences; lower is better for both. ECE rises from cautious to neutral to overconfident prompting in both families. AURC rises from cautious to overconfident prompting, and for DLMs it is flat between neutral and overconfident.
